🔒 Cisco Secure Email Encryption Service

How to Read Your Encrypted Emails - With Screenshots

Overview

Cisco Secure Email Encryption Service (CRES) provides secure, encrypted email communication. This guide walks you through accessing and reading your encrypted emails in just 7 simple steps with visual examples.

✓ Secure: Your encrypted messages are protected with industry-standard encryption.

1Receive a Secure Email Notification

When someone sends you an encrypted email, you will receive a notification email containing:

  • A secure message indicator with an envelope and lock icon
  • The message: "This is a secure message"
  • An expiration date and time for the message link
  • A blue "Read Message" button
⏰ Important: The link to open the encrypted message is time-limited. Open it before the expiration time displayed.

What to Do: Open the notification email in your inbox and click the "Read Message" button.

2Login or Register

Already Have an Account?

  3. Click "Log in"

First Time?

If you see an error message saying "You are not registered", click the "Register" button to create a new account.

3New User Registration

If you're not registered, you'll see an error. Click "Register" to create your account.

Personal Information

  • Email Address: Your recipient email address
  • First Name: Your first name
  • Last Name: Your last name

Create a Password

Your password must meet these requirements:

  • At least one alphabet character
  • At least one number
  • Between 8 and 40 characters in length
Important: Enter your password in both the "Current Password" and "Confirm Password" fields to ensure they match.

Complete Registration

  1. Check the checkbox: "I agree to Cisco Secure Email Encryption Service's Terms of Service"
  2. Click the "Register" button

7Access Your Encrypted Email

Now that your account is activated, you can read your encrypted email:

  1. Return to the original encrypted email notification in your inbox
  2. Click the "Read Message" button
  3. Enter your username (email address) and password
  4. Click "Log in"
  5. Your encrypted message will now be displayed
✓ Success! For all future encrypted emails, simply log in with your email and password to read them.

7bView Complete Encrypted Message

Troubleshooting

❓ Message link has expired
Contact the sender directly to request a new encrypted email or request a new message link if the expiration time has passed.
❓ Email not displaying correctly on mobile
Forward the notification email to: mobile@res.cisco.com
An alternative format will be sent to help you access it on your mobile device.
❓ Forgot your password?
On the login page, click "Forgot password?"
Reset instructions will be sent to your registered email address.
❓ Email address not found
Ensure you're using the correct email address that the sender used. Click "Email address not listed" to try a different email address. If you still have issues, create a new account or contact support.
❓ Can't find activation email
1. Check your junk or spam folder
2. Verify you entered the correct email address
3. Wait a few minutes and refresh your inbox
4. Contact your IT department or CRES support

🔒 Security Reminders

Never Share Your Password
Do not share your CRES password with anyone, including:
  • Cisco employees
  • IT support staff
  • Other colleagues
Message Links are Time-Limited
Always open encrypted emails before the expiration time to ensure you don't lose access.
Register Only Once
Your account persists across encrypted email messages. You don't need to register again for future emails.

Need Additional Help?
